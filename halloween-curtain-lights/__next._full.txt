<h2 id="the-honest-part-halloween-scenes-on-the-lumalyn-curtain">The honest part: Halloween scenes on the Lumalyn curtain</h2>
<p>Lumalyn lists 100+ animated scenes covering Christmas, snow and winter, festive messages, color patterns, ambient lighting and other holiday themes. The product page doesn&#39;t say which holidays those are, so we won&#39;t promise a ready-made jack-o&#39;-lantern.</p>
<p>What the specs do promise is DIY mode: you can draw your own pixel designs in the app or type scrolling text. That&#39;s how you build a Halloween show, and simple shapes are all it takes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Jack-o&#39;-lantern:</strong> a big orange circle with triangle eyes and a zigzag mouth left dark. Unlit pixels are your black.</li>
<li><strong>Ghost:</strong> a white rounded shape with two dark eyes against an unlit background.</li>
<li><strong>Bat and moon:</strong> a pale yellow disc with a bat shape left unlit in the middle.</li>
<li><strong>Messages:</strong> &quot;BOO!&quot;, &quot;TRICK OR TREAT&quot; or &quot;CANDY THIS WAY&quot; as scrolling text.</li>
<li><strong>Mood:</strong> an orange and purple color pattern on slow speed between your drawings.</li>
</ul>
<p>Bold, chunky designs read best on a grid of LEDs. The 6 ft 7 in (2 m) curtain packs its 400 LEDs into a tighter grid, about 100 per square meter versus about 44 on the 9 ft 10 in (3 m) size, so faces and letters look sharper.</p>
<h2 id="window-or-balcony-where-the-display-works-best">Window or balcony: where the display works best</h2>
<p><strong>In a window.</strong> Hanging the curtain inside the glass keeps it dry and close to an outlet, and a dark room behind it makes the colors pop from the street. One catch: text reads correctly from only one side of a light curtain. Lumalyn doesn&#39;t list a mirror setting, so scroll a short test word and check it from the sidewalk before Halloween night. Symmetrical pictures, like a pumpkin face, look fine either way. See /window-curtain-lights/ for glare and sizing tips.</p>
<p><strong>On a balcony.</strong> A balcony display is seen from farther away and by more people. Lumalyn rates the set IP44, and in the IP code that second 4 means protection against water splashing from any direction. So hang it where a roof or overhang keeps the rain off: it isn&#39;t made for open rain, and the USB plug must stay dry. Our /outdoor-curtain-lights/ guide covers sheltered spots in detail.</p>
<p><strong>On a porch or by the front door.</strong> Only under a covered porch, and with the cable kept well away from the path trick-or-treaters walk.</p>

<h2 id="safety-let-leds-replace-the-candles">Safety: let LEDs replace the candles</h2>
<p>Halloween mixes costumes, crowds and decorations, so a little care goes a long way. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends battery-operated lights and glow sticks instead of open-flame candles, using only lights tested by a recognized testing laboratory, and checking every set, new or old, for cracked sockets, frayed or bare wires and loose connections. It also advises clearing obstacles from lawns, steps and porches when trick-or-treaters are expected.</p>
<p>For a light curtain, that means:</p>
<ul>
<li>Inspect the curtain and cable when you unpack it, and again each year.</li>
<li>Run the USB cable where nobody can trip on it or snag a costume.</li>
<li>Keep the controller and USB plug indoors or fully under cover.</li>
<li>Because the curtain runs on 5 V USB, a power bank can light a covered porch without an extension cord across the walkway.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="when-to-order-and-why-it-isn-39-t-just-for-october">When to order (and why it isn&#39;t just for October)</h2>
<p>Timing matters more for Halloween than for Christmas. Lumalyn lists dispatch in 1–3 business days and delivery in about 7–12 business days after that, so allow up to about three weeks door to door. To have the set in your window by October 31, order by early October.</p>
<p>Missed that? The curtain still earns its keep. Lumalyn&#39;s order-by date for Christmas delivery is December 10, and the same set switches from your pumpkin to snow and winter scenes in a few taps. After that it&#39;s ready for birthdays, game nights and next October. Two sets are 10% off and three are 20% off, which suits a matching window and balcony.</p>

</ul>
