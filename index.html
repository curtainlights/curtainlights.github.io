§ 6 · Questions

Curtain lights FAQ

What are curtain lights?

Curtain lights are a top wire with many thin strands of LEDs hanging down, so one set covers a whole window, wall or balcony railing with light. Plain sets glow or twinkle; pixel (animated) sets control every LED and can show pictures and text.

What size curtain lights do I need?

Measure the width you want to cover and pick the closest size without going over. Lumalyn’s pixel curtain comes in 2 × 2 m (6 ft 7 in) for windows and short railings, and 3 × 3 m (9 ft 10 in) for wide railings and patio doors.

Can curtain lights go outside?

Only if they are rated for it. IP44 sets suit covered balconies, porches and windows; spots that take direct rain need IP65 or higher. Keep plugs and adapters dry.

Do animated curtain lights need Wi-Fi?

Not always. The Lumalyn curtain uses a Bluetooth app plus a remote, so there is no Wi-Fi or router setup; you control it from nearby.

Are curtain lights only for Christmas?

No. Warm-white curtains work all year in bedrooms and windows, and pixel curtains switch between Christmas scenes, other holiday themes, party colors and calm ambient light.
