What LED curtain lights are

An LED curtain is a row of thin light strands hanging from one top cable, a bit like a bead curtain made of tiny bulbs. You hang the top line from hooks, a rod or a frame, and the strands drop down to fill a rectangle with light.

Because it covers an area rather than a line, a curtain fills a window or a glass balcony in a way a single string never can. It also reads well from a distance. That's why curtains are a favorite for street-facing windows, balcony glass, party backdrops and bedroom walls.

Modern sets fall into two families, and picking between them is the biggest decision you'll make.

Plain fairy curtains vs. pixel (animated) curtains

Plain fairy curtain Pixel (animated) curtain
How LEDs are switched All together, or in a few groups Each LED on its own
What it can show Steady glow, twinkle, fade, chase Pictures, scrolling text, animations, color patterns
Colors Usually one color or a fixed mix Typically full RGB, any color on any LED
Controls Often a button box or basic remote App, remote, music sync, timer
Best for Soft background glow A display people stop and watch

A plain fairy curtain is lovely behind sheer drapes or above a bed. Many sets offer a handful of preset modes, and that's the whole menu.

A pixel curtain treats every LED as a dot on a large, soft screen. That's what lets it draw a tree, a heart or a name. It costs more than a basic string, but one curtain can carry you through Christmas, a birthday, game night and Halloween instead of sitting in a box eleven months a year.

If you only want a warm glow, start with our curtain fairy lights guide. If you want a window that changes with the calendar, a pixel curtain is the better buy.

Size and LED count: why density matters

Curtains are sold by width × drop. You'll often see sizes from about 3 ft 3 in (1 m) square up to 9 ft 10 in (3 m) square, with the total LED count listed beside the size.

For a plain curtain, more LEDs mostly means more glow. For a pixel curtain, the number to watch is density: how many LEDs share each square foot. Pictures and letters look crisper when the dots sit closer together.

Power: plug-in, USB or battery

Curtain lights run on low voltage, but they get it in different ways:

  • Mains adapter. Many plain curtains come with a plug-in transformer. Simple, but you need an outlet close by.
  • USB. A growing number of sets, Lumalyn's included, take 5 V USB. Any phone charger works, and so does a power bank, which is handy on a balcony with no outlet.
  • Batteries. Some small curtains run on AA cells. Fine for one party, tiresome for nightly use.

One thing to know up front: Lumalyn does not include a USB adapter in the box. Most homes have a spare phone charger in a drawer, but check before the curtain arrives so the first evening isn't spent hunting for one. The remote also needs AAA batteries, which aren't included.

Weather ratings: where you can hang them

Outdoor use comes down to the IP rating. The second digit tells you how well the set keeps water out:

Rating Water protection Good for
IP20 None Indoors only
IP44 Splashing from any direction Indoors, covered balconies, porches, inside windows
IP65 Water jets Exposed spots in open rain
IP67 Temporary immersion Low spots where water can pool

Controls: remote, app, music and timer

How you control a curtain decides whether you'll still enjoy it in week three.

  • Remote: fast on/off and mode changes from the sofa.
  • App: browse scenes, pick colors, set brightness and speed.
  • Music sync: the lights move with the music in the room.
  • Timer: the curtain switches on and off by itself.

Lumalyn pairs a Bluetooth app for iOS and Android with a remote. No Wi-Fi network is needed. The trade-off is that Bluetooth works from nearby, so you control it while you're home rather than from across town. For a window or balcony, that's usually when you're enjoying it anyway. Frequently asked questions

Are LED curtain lights worth it?

If you want a window or balcony that people notice, yes. A curtain fills an area instead of tracing a line, so it reads clearly from the street. Pixel curtains add the most value because one set can cover Christmas, birthdays, parties and other occasions all year.

How many LEDs should a curtain light have?

It depends on the size and the job. For a soft glow the count matters less. For pictures and text, look at density: Lumalyn's 400 LEDs work out to about 9 per sq ft (100 per m²) on the 6 ft 7 in (2 m) set and about 4 per sq ft (44 per m²) on the 9 ft 10 in (3 m) set.

Can LED curtain lights be used outside?

Only where the rating allows. IP44 sets like Lumalyn's suit covered balconies, porches and the inside of windows. Spots exposed to open rain call for IP65 or higher, and plugs and chargers must always stay dry.

Do LED curtain lights use a lot of electricity?

LEDs typically draw far less power than old incandescent mini lights. Lumalyn's curtain runs on 5 V USB, so a standard phone charger or power bank can drive it. Lumalyn does not publish a wattage figure.

What is the difference between fairy curtain lights and pixel curtain lights?

Fairy curtains switch all their LEDs together or in a few groups, so they glow, twinkle or fade. Pixel curtains control each LED on its own, which lets them draw pictures, scroll text and play animations.
