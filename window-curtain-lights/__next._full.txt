<p>The weather row decides it. Lumalyn rates its curtain IP44. In the IP code, that second 4 means protection against water splashing from any direction, a step below the water-jet protection of IPX5 or IP65. <h2 id="glare-and-reflections-making-the-glass-work-for-you">Glare and reflections: making the glass work for you</h2>
<p>At night, a lit room turns a window into a mirror, and a curtain of LEDs can show up twice: once for real and once reflected. A few habits fix most of it:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hang close to the glass.</strong> The nearer the curtain is to the pane, the closer its reflection sits to the real LEDs, so the doubling is much less noticeable.</li>
<li><strong>Dim the room.</strong> Turn off overhead lights so people outside see the curtain rather than your living room, and you see less reflection from inside.</li>
<li><strong>Lower the brightness for indoor viewing.</strong> A pixel curtain at full brightness a few feet from the sofa can be dazzling. Lumalyn lists adjustable brightness, so turn it down in the evening.</li>
<li><strong>Test text from outside.</strong> A light curtain is visible from both sides, but text reads correctly from only one. Lumalyn doesn&#39;t list a mirror setting, so scroll a test word and look from the sidewalk before you rely on messages. Symmetrical pictures look fine either way.</li>
</ul>
<p>Double-glazed windows can show a faint second reflection. That&#39;s ordinary glass behavior, not a fault in the lights.</p>

<p>Height is the other half. Each curtain is as tall as it is wide, so on a window shorter than the curtain, the strands will run past the sill. Let them hang toward the floor if you can. Bunching or folding a pixel curtain distorts its pictures and text, because each LED has a fixed spot in the grid. The 2 m size also gives the sharper image, at about 100 LEDs/m² versus about 44 on the 3 m set. More measuring help is in the /curtain-lights-size-guide/.</p>
<h2 id="working-with-blinds-and-drapes">Working with blinds and drapes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Between the glass and blinds.</strong> Mount the curtain on a tension rod inside the window recess or on hooks along the top of the frame, then lower the blinds behind it. From outside, the closed blinds become a clean backdrop; inside, they block the glow at bedtime. Check that the blinds still move freely.</li>
<li><strong>Behind sheer curtains.</strong> Hang the lights against the glass with the sheers in front. Inside you get a soft, diffused glow, and the street still sees the full picture.</li>
<li><strong>With heavy drapes.</strong> Use the inner track of a double rod or a separate rod so the drapes still close over the lights. It&#39;s a cozy combination for a bedroom; see /curtain-lights-for-bedroom/.</li>
</ul>
<p>Keep the USB controller and charger where air can circulate, not wedged against a radiator or buried in fabric.</p>
<h2 id="condensation-cold-glass-and-wet-sills">Condensation, cold glass and wet sills</h2>
<p>Most condensation in a home forms when warm, moist air meets a cool surface, and it&#39;s especially noticeable in winter when heating meets cold glass, above all single glazing. The result is beads of water on the pane and puddles on the sill, often first thing in the morning.</p>
<ul>
<li>Don&#39;t let the strands rest against wet glass; a small gap helps.</li>
<li>Keep the USB controller, plug and charger off the sill, where water collects. Lumalyn says the USB plug must stay dry, and a phone charger isn&#39;t built for damp sills at all.</li>
<li>If condensation is heavy, better ventilation or a dehumidifier helps the room as well as the lights.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="power-and-control-at-the-window">Power and control at the window</h2>
<p>The curtain runs on 5 V USB from any USB charger or power bank, though the adapter isn&#39;t included. A charger in a nearby outlet and the cable run neatly along the frame keep things tidy. Control is by Bluetooth app (iOS and Android) or the included remote, from nearby, with no Wi-Fi needed.</p>
<p>The feature that makes a window display effortless is the timer. Lumalyn lists one, so the curtain can switch on in the early evening and off at bedtime every day without anyone touching it. For a deeper look at the app, see /smart-curtain-lights/.</p>
